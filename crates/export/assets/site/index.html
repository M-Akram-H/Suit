    VRewind: the slide before, backwards. Next plays it forwards again.
    RThis slide again, from its start.
    SpaceHold, and carry on.
    Home  EndThe first slide, the last slide.
    FFull screen on or off.
    NThe notes of this slide.
    B  .Blank the screen, and back.
    HHide or show the mouse pointer.
    ?These keys.
